# Kickoff call with Northshore

Kickoff for Northshore's Zoho CRM implementation. Mark walked through how leads arrive today and where they get lost, and Priya described her intake routine. Most of the call was lead intake and referral tracking; quoting came up briefly at the end.

**Attendees:** Mark Tanaka (Owner/GM, Northshore), Priya Shah (Office Admin, Northshore), Len Kamerman (Hero), Linda (Hero)

## Needs

- All leads in one place, whatever the source (Mark). Today they're split between a shared spreadsheet and the Gmail inbox.
- Lead source recorded without anyone typing it in, so Mark can trust the source numbers.
- Referral leads record who referred them, because partners get a $250 thank-you when a referred job closes (Mark).
- Mark wants to see which partners actually send business, and how often.
- Priya needs to enter phone and email referrals quickly; she currently retypes them from her notebook.
- First response to every lead within one business day (Mark).

## Constraints

- Peak season is April to November; go-live needs to land before April 2027 (Mark).
- Two referral partner firms today: a landscape design firm and a pool installer.
- The website form is on northshoreoutdoor.ca and is managed by an outside web developer.

## Proposed approaches

- Priya suggested a "referred by" text box on the lead form.
- Mark asked whether the website form could "just send straight into the CRM."

## Decisions made

- Partners stay at $250 per closed referral for 2027 (Mark). (resolves SRC-001-Q2)

## Open questions

- **SRC-002-Q1:** Who updates the website form: Hero or the outside developer?
- **SRC-002-Q2:** Should past clients who refer get any thank-you? Mark said "maybe a gift card" but didn't commit.
- **SRC-002-Q3:** How are leads from the Facebook and Instagram ads getting to Northshore today? Not discussed.
- **SRC-002-Q4:** No one owns follow-up when a lead isn't reached in the first day.

## Key quotes

- Mark: "I have no idea if the pool guys have sent us two jobs or twenty."
- Mark: "If it's not in by April, it's not happening until next fall."
- Priya: "Half my morning is copying names out of my notebook."

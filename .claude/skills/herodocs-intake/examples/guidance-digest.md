# Hero guidance on quoting approach

Len's direction on how Northshore's quoting should be approached, written after the kickoff call and the review of their quote template. It addresses the client document's suggestion to build quoting in Creator.

## Direction

- **Build quoting in CRM, using Quotes, not in Creator.** Quotes have to stay tied to the Deal for the pipeline and conversion reports Mark wants, and keeping them in CRM avoids a sync between two apps for a team of five. (resolves SRC-001-Q6)
- **Handle quote versions as separate Quotes linked to the same Deal**, rather than a custom versioning module. Simpler to build and to report on, and Northshore's revision volume is low.
- **Use Zoho Sign for e-signature** instead of a third-party tool. Northshore already pays for Zoho One.
- **Hero builds the website form; Northshore's developer embeds it.** (resolves SRC-002-Q1)
- **Out of scope for phase 1:** crew scheduling and materials ordering. Dan keeps his current process; phase 1 only notifies him when a job is sold.
